## An interesting thing

<!-- more -->
Today, just like usual, I set a half-hour alarm for my nap. But it was raining, and when the alarm went off, my mind was blank, and I didn’t think much, just turned over and went back to sleep. This went on, and I ended up sleeping for two hours. 
Occasionally zoning out feels really nice, like my whole body is relaxed. Honestly, it’s quite refreshing. That’s why I felt like recording this moment.
